# Hand-check — the answer key against printed debt tables

> Generated by `scripts/handcheck.py grade`. Each transcription (`manifests/handcheck/<episode>.json`) was read from the report's printed debt table, not from its XBRL tags, and graded *as a ledger* against the answer key. Agreement means the key, the grader and the printed report say the same thing.

## Calibration set (DESIGN.md §11's 10 company-quarters): 10 of 10 agree

The first grading of the calibration set agreed on **8 of 10**. Every disagreement was traced to
its cause, and each was a defect in the answer key or the grader, not in the printed report:

| # | Found on | Defect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| P30 | Oracle 10-K | Repaid notes kept their original face amount tagged at the period end, with carrying amount 0; the key counted them as outstanding | An explicit zero balance wins; face at period end counts only when no balance is tagged |
| P31 | AMD | A member naming two bonds ("4.319% … Due 2028 And 4.212% … Due 2026") was read as a duplicate 4.319% 2028 bond | Members naming several coupons or maturities are deals, not bonds |
| P32 | Broadcom | Debt assumed in the VMware acquisition is tagged on a `BusinessAcquisition` axis, which the key discarded (56 facts, 10 notes) | The axis is descriptive, so the facts are kept |
| P33 | Broadcom | VMware's notes are named by due date (`May2025SeniorNotes`) and were read as issue-date deals | A month on or after the report's period end is a maturity; and one member is misspelt `SeniorNots` |
| P34 | Equinix | A Swiss franc note's label never names the currency and both its amounts are tagged in USD; the key called it a dollar bond | A currency counts as known only from the label or a foreign-unit face; otherwise it does not block a match |
| — | Microsoft | An offering's tagged face is the *original* deal size ($19.8 bn), not what is left ($7.93 bn) | Offerings are graded on their outstanding carrying amount |

These ten were used to find the fixes, so their 10 of 10 is a calibration, not an independent

## Review notes

- **Equinix 10-K 20241231** — Rows 30-57 (issued, maturity, Dec 31 2024 amount, effective rate, prior year). Foreign notes are printed as US-dollar equivalents, so their own-currency principal is not on the page: currency recorded, principal unscored. The 2.625% 2024 notes show '—' (repaid). Rows 1-29 are the cover page and exhibit list.
- **Nvidia 10-K 20250131** — Rows 11-18 of the long-term debt table (remaining term, effective rate, Jan 26 2025, Jan 28 2024); the 0.584% 2024 notes show '—' (repaid). Net carrying amount $8,463 m.
- **Broadcom 10-Q 20250430** — Rows 1-44 (rate, effective rate, current quarter, prior year end); '—' means repaid (3.125% 2025 notes, the 2026 term loan). The 4.489% loan and both SOFR term loans are loans, not bonds. Rows 48+ are the exhibit list.
- **Micron 10-Q 20250531** — Rows 16-29 (stated rate, effective rate, current, long-term, total; then the prior period). Amounts are net carrying amounts, within 1% of face. The 2029 Term Loan A is a loan, not a bond. Two notes share 5.875% / 2033 (A due February, B due September) and differ in amount.
- **CoreWeave 10-Q 20250630** — The debt table (in thousands): one bond — the 9.250% senior notes due June 2030 (the % column printed beside it is the effective rate), $2.0 bn issued May 2025 — plus three facilities and OEM financing. Total debt, net $11,051.5 m; non-current $7,423.8 m, so current $3,627.7 m.
- **Oracle 10-K 20250531** — Rows 2-56 (face, coupon and maturity in the label; issued; May 31 2025; effective rate; prior year). '—' means repaid: the July and November 2024 notes (which the answer key had resurrected from their face tags — fixed, P30), the April and May 2025 notes, and the 2022 SOFR term loans. Floating notes have no fixed coupon. The SOFR + 1.35% line is the June 2024 term loan, 'other borrowings' is not a bond. The euro note's principal is printed in euros (€750 m). Total notes payable and other borrowings $92,568 m.
- **Salesforce 10-Q 20250731** — Rows 1-6: instrument, issue date, maturity, coupon (its own cell, no % sign), aggregate principal. Rows 7-12 repeat the same notes in a second table and are not counted twice.
- **Microsoft 10-K 20250630** — The debt table is by issuance year with coupon ranges (no single coupon per row), outstanding face at June 30 2025: 13 issuances, total face $49,206 m. The 2013 euro issuance (€4.1 bn issued) is printed at its US-dollar value, $2,700 m. Total debt $43,151 m (after $4,864 m premium on debt exchange and discounts); current portion $2,999 m.
- **Meta 10-K 20241231** — Rows 2-4: each offering (maturity range, coupon range, effective range, Dec 31 2024, Dec 31 2023). August 2024 notes $10,500 m are new this year.
- **Vistra 10-Q 20250630** — Totals only (the episode's level): 'Total long-term debt including amounts due currently' $15,770 m and 'Less amounts due currently' $230 m. The forward repurchase obligation ($1,295 m) is printed as a separate line and is not debt.
- **AMD 10-Q 20250331** — Rows 1-5 (principal at Mar 29 2025, Dec 28 2024). The two 2025 notes ($875 m 4.212% 2026, $625 m 4.319% 2028) are new this quarter. Total long-term debt (net) $3,217 m.
- **Digital Realty 10-Q 20250630** — Rows 4-25 of the senior notes table (in thousands): principal in the borrowed currency, its US-dollar amount, maturity date, balance at Jun 30 2025 and Dec 31 2024. The £400 m 4.250% 2025 notes show '—' (repaid in January); the €650 m 0.625% notes were still outstanding at Jun 30 (paid July 15). Euro notes identified as rows whose principal differs from its dollar amount (the € cell is stripped in extraction).
